You are the blind weight-space writer. Two endpoint policies repeatedly steer a robot to a target. A brief sideways command disturbance can displace the robot; both specialists have a feedback response. Can you identify that response in both policies and adjust its strength so that a single policy recovers rapidly between the endpoint conditions? Use only this packet and local numerical tools. Final simulator trials are held by an independent evaluator.

Read policy.py for the exact forward pass. A.npz and B.npz contain the specialists. initial.npz is the common initialization. Inputs are six anonymized sensor channels; outputs are three continuous actions. Each traces file has observations and commanded actions with shape (20 scenarios, 3 matched rollouts, 25 steps, channels). The rollouts are in order: undisturbed, negative sideways pulse, positive sideways pulse. A pulse overrides the sideways command at pulse_start and pulse_start+1. The first opportunity to correct occurs at pulse_start+2. The logged actions are the policy's intended actions. Endpoint scenario IDs are paired across policies. probes.npz gives both policies' actions on the same 1,024 nearby inputs. Use supplied traces and probes as the only source of teacher labels for fitting. You may inspect weights, activations, sensitivities and proposed interventions on these inputs; do not create additional teacher training labels or fetch other data or simulators.

Submit one submission.zip containing exactly:
- neutral.npz: a single combined controller at its unamplified setting.
- delta.npz: a localized change intended to tune the proposed shared response. Grader runs neutral minus delta, neutral, and neutral plus delta. Arrays in both NPZ files have exactly the six names and shapes of A.npz and finite floating-point values. Keep delta nonzero in at most 10% of parameters. The intended amplified submission is neutral plus delta.
- mask.npz: boolean arrays with those six names and shapes. Nominate the weights in the two original specialists that implement the shared response. Use a common set of indices for this common-initialization pair, one to 10% of parameters. The scorer will restore these indices to their initial values and compare matched random masks.
- prediction.txt: identify the shared computation; give evidence in each specialist; describe and justify your edit; commit to the direction of the first corrective action under each push, the ordering of recovery errors across the three settings, and any expected overshoot. Predict how restoring the nominated weights should affect each original specialist. Offer a result that would falsify your account.

The main outcome is lateral return toward the matched undisturbed trajectory two actions after pulse release (within 2 cm). We also check end-of-episode goal success and the signed corrective action relative to the undisturbed command at the first opportunity to react. Endpoints, intermediate conditions, timings, and goal variations will be scored once. Submit a single frozen edit without feedback.
